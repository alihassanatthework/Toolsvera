About the Word Counter

The ToolsVera word counter tallies words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences and paragraphs as you type or paste. Essential for essays with strict limits, social posts with character caps and SEO content targeting a specific length.

How to use the Word Counter

  1. Paste your text: Type or paste any content into the text area.
  2. View live counts: Totals update instantly while you edit.
  3. Adjust as needed: Trim or expand your content to hit the target.
