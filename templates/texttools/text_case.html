Does title case capitalize every word?

Title case capitalizes the first letter of each significant word, leaving short articles like “and” lowercase.

Is inverse case supported?

Yes — lowercase becomes uppercase and vice versa, letter by letter.

Will it affect symbols and numbers?

No. Non-letter characters are preserved exactly as-is.
