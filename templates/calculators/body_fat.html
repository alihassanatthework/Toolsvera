About the Body Fat Calculator

The ToolsVera body fat calculator estimates your body fat percentage using the well-known US Navy tape method. It is a practical no-equipment alternative to calipers and scale scans for tracking progress during fat loss or recomposition.

How to use the Body Fat Calculator

  1. Measure with a tape: Measure neck, waist (and hips for women) with a flexible tape.
  2. Enter your numbers: Type measurements along with height and sex.
  3. Read your BF%: Your estimated body fat percentage appears instantly.

How accurate is the Navy method?

It is typically within a few percentage points of DEXA for most adults — very usable for tracking trends.

Measure in the morning before eating for the most consistent week-to-week results.
