About the Discount Calculator

The ToolsVera discount calculator works out the final sale price and the amount you save given an original price and a percent-off discount. Perfect for shopping, black-Friday planning and store owners quickly pricing items.

How to use the Discount Calculator

  1. Enter the original price: Type the price before the discount.
  2. Enter the discount percent: Add the percent-off value (e.g. 25 for 25% off).
  3. See sale price: Final price and savings appear instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Discount Calculator really free?

Yes. Discount Calculator on ToolsVera is 100% free to use with no signup, no hidden limits and no watermark added to your output.

How do I stack two discounts?

Apply the first discount, then run the calculator again on the result using the second percent.

Does it include sales tax?

No. Use our Tax Calculator on the discounted price to add local tax.

Can it reverse from sale price to original?

Yes — given the sale price and percent off, you can derive the original with simple algebra or by using percent change.
