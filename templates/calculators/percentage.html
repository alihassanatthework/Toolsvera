What is the difference between % change and % difference?

Percent change compares a new value to an original value. Percent difference compares two values relative to their average.

How do I calculate a discount?

For a 20% discount on $50, compute 20% of 50 = $10, so the sale price is $40. Our Discount Calculator does this in one step.

Can negative percentages appear?

Yes — a decrease shows as a negative percent change.
