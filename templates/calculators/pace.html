Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Pace Calculator really free?

Yes. Pace Calculator on ToolsVera is 100% free to use with no signup, no hidden limits and no watermark added to your output.

Is pace the same as speed?

Pace is time per distance (e.g. 5:00/km), while speed is distance per time (e.g. 12 km/h). They are inverse views of the same run.

What is a good marathon pace?

Beginners often target 6:00–7:00/km; competitive amateurs aim for sub-5:00/km — always train up to race pace safely.

Can I use it for cycling?

Cycling usually uses speed (km/h) instead of pace. Our Speed Calculator is a better fit there.

Is my data safe?
