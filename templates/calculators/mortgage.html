About the Mortgage Calculator

The ToolsVera mortgage calculator estimates your monthly home loan payment from the loan amount, interest rate and term. Use it to compare offers, stress-test your budget or see how extra down payment changes your monthly cost.

How to use the Mortgage Calculator

  1. Enter the loan amount: Type the total amount you plan to borrow.
  2. Enter rate and term: Add the annual interest rate and the loan length in years.
  3. See your payment: Get your estimated monthly payment instantly.

Why use our Mortgage Calculator?

  • Fast estimate of principal + interest per month.
  • Helps compare 15-year vs 30-year mortgages.
  • Free forever — no lender signup required.
  • Useful for buyers, refinancers and real-estate planning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Mortgage Calculator really free?

Yes. Mortgage Calculator on ToolsVera is 100% free to use with no signup, no hidden limits and no watermark added to your output.

Does this include taxes and insurance?

No. The calculator shows principal and interest only. Add property tax and insurance separately for your full PITI.

Is the result exact to the dollar of my lender’s quote?

It is an accurate standard amortization estimate. Lender quotes may differ slightly due to fees and rounding.

Can I calculate biweekly payments?

For biweekly payments, halve the monthly result — you will pay one extra month per year that way.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Files are processed on the server and automatically deleted — we never store your content.
