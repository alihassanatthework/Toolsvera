About the Grade Calculator

The ToolsVera grade calculator tells you your current weighted grade and exactly what you need on the final exam to reach a target grade. Great for the last two weeks of any semester when you are scrambling for a borderline A.

How to use the Grade Calculator

  1. Enter current grades: Add each assignment grade and its weight.
  2. Set your target: Type the final grade you are aiming for.
  3. See what you need: The calculator shows the score required on your final.

Why use our Grade Calculator?

  • Clear picture of where you stand right now.
  • Shows exactly what is achievable and what is not.
  • Supports any weighting scheme your class uses.
  • Free, no login, no ads.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Grade Calculator really free?

Yes. Grade Calculator on ToolsVera is 100% free to use with no signup, no hidden limits and no watermark added to your output.

What if the required score is above 100?

That means the target is mathematically unreachable given the final’s weight. Aim for the next grade tier instead.

Can I include extra credit?

Add it as an extra assignment with the weight your professor assigned to extra credit.

Does it work with pass/fail courses?

Pass/fail courses are not graded numerically; this tool targets letter/percentage grading systems.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Files are processed on the server and automatically deleted — we never store your content.
