About the Calorie Calculator

The ToolsVera calorie calculator estimates how many calories you need per day based on your age, sex, height, weight and activity level. It is a solid starting point for building a meal plan for maintenance, fat loss or muscle gain.

How to use the Calorie Calculator

  1. Enter your stats: Provide your age, height, weight and sex.
  2. Pick activity level: Choose how active your typical week is.
  3. Read daily calories: See TDEE plus targets for loss and gain.

Why use our Calorie Calculator?

  • Science-based Mifflin-St Jeor BMR formula.
  • Gives maintenance, cutting and bulking targets.
  • Quick adjustments for activity level changes.
  • Private — no account, no tracking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Calorie Calculator really free?

Yes. Calorie Calculator on ToolsVera is 100% free to use with no signup, no hidden limits and no watermark added to your output.

Is this a replacement for a nutritionist?

No. It is a helpful estimate. For medical or performance nutrition, consult a registered dietitian.

What activity level should I pick?

Most desk workers without structured exercise are “sedentary” or “lightly active”. Choose “very active” only if you train hard most days.

How often should I recalculate?

Recalculate when your weight changes by 4–5 kg or your activity level shifts substantially.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Files are processed on the server and automatically deleted — we never store your content.
