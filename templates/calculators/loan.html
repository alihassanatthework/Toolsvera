Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Loan Calculator really free?

Yes. Loan Calculator on ToolsVera is 100% free to use with no signup, no hidden limits and no watermark added to your output.

What is EMI?

EMI means Equated Monthly Installment — a fixed amount paid every month including principal and interest.

Can I include processing fees?

Add the fee to your loan amount if it is financed into the loan; otherwise treat it as a one-time upfront cost.

Does it support fortnightly or weekly payments?

The tool computes monthly EMI. Divide by 2 or 4 for an approximate fortnightly or weekly figure.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Files are processed on the server and automatically deleted — we never store your content.
