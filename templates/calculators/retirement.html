About the Retirement Calculator

The ToolsVera retirement calculator projects how your savings grow until retirement based on your current balance, annual contributions, expected return and time horizon. Perfect for early career planners and mid-career checkpoints.

How to use the Retirement Calculator

  1. Enter current savings: Type what you already have invested for retirement.
  2. Add contributions: Include your yearly contribution.
  3. Set time and return: Enter years to retirement and expected annual return.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Retirement Calculator really free?

Yes. Retirement Calculator on ToolsVera is 100% free to use with no signup, no hidden limits and no watermark added to your output.

What return rate should I use?

A long-term balanced portfolio historically returns 6–8% nominal. Be conservative in your planning and use 5–6% as a baseline.

Does it account for inflation?

Use a real return (nominal minus expected inflation) to see inflation-adjusted values.

Does it include taxes?

No. Results are pre-tax estimates — adjust for your country’s retirement account rules.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Files are processed on the server and automatically deleted — we never store your content.
