About the Compound Interest Calculator

The ToolsVera compound interest calculator projects how your savings or investments grow when interest compounds over months or years. Tweak the rate, term and contributions to visualize the power of long-term investing.

How to use the Compound Interest Calculator

  1. Enter your principal: Start with the amount you are investing today.
  2. Add rate and time: Enter the annual interest rate and the number of years.
  3. View the future value: See the total value and interest earned over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Compound Interest Calculator really free?

Yes. Compound Interest Calculator on ToolsVera is 100% free to use with no signup, no hidden limits and no watermark added to your output.

Monthly or annual compounding — which is better?

More frequent compounding yields slightly higher returns. The difference is small for everyday savings but adds up over decades.

Does this account for inflation?

No. The calculator shows nominal growth. Subtract your expected inflation rate for a real-terms estimate.

Can I add regular contributions?

This simplified version uses a one-time principal. For recurring deposits, increase the principal or run multiple scenarios.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Files are processed on the server and automatically deleted — we never store your content.
