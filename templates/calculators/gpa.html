About the GPA Calculator

The ToolsVera GPA calculator computes your semester or cumulative grade point average from your course grades and credit hours. It is perfect for students tracking progress, applying to scholarships or planning how much a tough class could move the needle.

How to use the GPA Calculator

  1. Add each course: Enter the grade and credit hours for every course.
  2. Keep adding rows: Add as many courses as you need for the term.
  3. See your GPA: Your weighted GPA updates instantly.

Why use our GPA Calculator?

  • Weighted by credit hours for accuracy.
  • Great for semester or cumulative GPA views.
  • No signup, no tracking.
  • Works on phone and laptop equally well.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this GPA Calculator really free?

Yes. GPA Calculator on ToolsVera is 100% free to use with no signup, no hidden limits and no watermark added to your output.

What GPA scale is used?

Standard US 4.0 scale (A = 4.0, B = 3.0, C = 2.0, D = 1.0, F = 0.0) with +/- modifiers.

How do I calculate cumulative GPA?

Enter every course from every term you want included and the calculator weights them all together.

Can I add weighted honors/AP courses?

Treat an honors A as 4.5 or 5.0 if your school uses weighted GPA — otherwise use the standard scale.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Files are processed on the server and automatically deleted — we never store your content.
