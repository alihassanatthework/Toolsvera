What is a healthy BMI range?

For adults, the World Health Organization considers a BMI of 18.5–24.9 as the healthy range.

Does BMI work for athletes?

BMI does not distinguish muscle from fat, so highly muscular athletes can appear overweight even when they are healthy.

Is BMI accurate for children?

Children should use age- and sex-specific BMI-for-age charts instead of the adult cutoffs.
