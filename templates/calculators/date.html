About the Date Calculator

The ToolsVera date calculator finds the number of days between two dates or adds and subtracts days from a given date. Perfect for deadlines, contracts, pregnancy tracking and trip planning.

How to use the Date Calculator

  1. Pick your dates: Select the start and end dates, or a base date and an offset.
  2. Choose the operation: Measure the difference or shift by days.
  3. See the result: Duration or target date displays instantly.

Is this Date Calculator really free?

Yes. Date Calculator on ToolsVera is 100% free to use with no signup, no hidden limits and no watermark added to your output.

Does it count inclusive or exclusive days?

Standard difference is exclusive of the start date. Add 1 if your context counts both dates.

Does it handle time zones?

The calculator uses whole calendar days and ignores time zones.

Can I add months or years directly?

This version works in days. Add approximate months (30 days) for quick estimates.
