The ToolsVera percentage change calculator tells you how much a value increased or decreased relative to its starting point. It is ideal for comparing sales, stock prices, weight loss progress and before/after metrics of any kind.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Percentage Change Calculator really free?

Yes. Percentage Change Calculator on ToolsVera is 100% free to use with no signup, no hidden limits and no watermark added to your output.

What if the original value is zero?

Percent change is undefined when dividing by zero. Use a small baseline or a different metric.

Is percent change the same as percent difference?

No. Percent change is relative to the original value; percent difference divides by the average of the two values.

Can the result be more than 100%?

Yes — if a value more than doubles, percent increase exceeds 100%.
