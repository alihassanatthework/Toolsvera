Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Sleep Calculator really free?

Yes. Sleep Calculator on ToolsVera is 100% free to use with no signup, no hidden limits and no watermark added to your output.

Is every sleep cycle exactly 90 minutes?

Cycles average 90 minutes but vary 70–110 minutes. Treat suggestions as a helpful baseline, not an exact science.

How many cycles should I aim for?

Five to six cycles (7.5–9 hours) is a common target for most adults.

Does caffeine affect this?

Yes. Avoid caffeine 6+ hours before bed to keep cycles undisturbed.
