About the Pregnancy Calculator

The ToolsVera pregnancy calculator estimates your due date and current week of pregnancy based on your last menstrual period. It is a popular first tool for expectant parents before formal ultrasound dating.

How to use the Pregnancy Calculator

  1. Enter your LMP: Pick the first day of your last menstrual period.
  2. Adjust cycle length: Use 28 days by default or enter your own.
  3. See your due date: The calculator shows due date and current week.

Why use our Pregnancy Calculator?

  • Standard Naegele’s rule with cycle-length adjustment.
  • Shows current pregnancy week at a glance.
  • Private — nothing is saved.
  • Free and easy to reshare with your partner.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Pregnancy Calculator really free?

Yes. Pregnancy Calculator on ToolsVera is 100% free to use with no signup, no hidden limits and no watermark added to your output.

Is this a replacement for a doctor?

No — always confirm due dates with your healthcare provider, especially after ultrasound dating.

What if my cycle is not 28 days?

Enter your actual cycle length; the calculator adjusts the ovulation offset accordingly.

Does it work for IVF pregnancies?

For IVF, dating is based on embryo transfer — consult your clinic’s schedule instead of LMP.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Files are processed on the server and automatically deleted — we never store your content.
