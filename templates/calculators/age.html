About the Age Calculator

The ToolsVera age calculator tells you your exact age in years, months and days from your date of birth. It is perfect for forms, eligibility checks and just satisfying curiosity about milestones between two dates.

How to use the Age Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth: Pick the day, month and year you were born.
  2. Choose the target date: Use today or any reference date you want to measure against.
  3. View your exact age: See the result broken down into years, months and days.

Why use our Age Calculator?

  • Accurate calendar math — handles leap years correctly.
  • Breaks age into years, months and days, not just years.
  • No signup and no data is stored.
  • Great for school forms, ID applications and milestones.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Age Calculator really free?

Yes. Age Calculator on ToolsVera is 100% free to use with no signup, no hidden limits and no watermark added to your output.

Does the calculator handle leap years?

Yes. February 29 birthdays and intervening leap days are handled correctly in the day count.

Can I calculate age for a future date?

Yes. Use any target date — past or future — to see how old you were or will be.

Is my birth date stored anywhere?

No. The calculation runs on the server for this request only and nothing is saved.

Yes. Files are processed on the server and automatically deleted — we never store your content.
