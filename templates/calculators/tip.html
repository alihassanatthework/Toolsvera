About the Tip Calculator

The ToolsVera tip calculator works out the tip and each person’s share of the bill in seconds. Perfect for restaurants, cafes and group dinners where you want to split evenly without awkward math.

How to use the Tip Calculator

  1. Enter the bill total: Type the pre-tip amount on the check.
  2. Pick a tip percentage: Choose 10%, 15%, 18%, 20% or a custom value.
  3. Split between people: Enter the number of diners to see each person’s share.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Tip Calculator really free?

Yes. Tip Calculator on ToolsVera is 100% free to use with no signup, no hidden limits and no watermark added to your output.

Should I tip on pre-tax or post-tax total?

Tip is usually calculated on the pre-tax subtotal, though many people simply tip on the final total for convenience.

What is a standard tip percentage?

In the US, 18–20% is standard for good service. Other countries have different norms — tip what feels right locally.

Can it handle uneven splits?

This calculator assumes an even split. For uneven shares, divide the items manually and apply tip proportionally.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Files are processed on the server and automatically deleted — we never store your content.
