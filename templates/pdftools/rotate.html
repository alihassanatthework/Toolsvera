About the Rotate PDF

Rotate PDF pages online to fix sideways scans and landscape pages that show upside down. ToolsVera lets you rotate every page by 90°, 180° or 270° in one click so your documents read correctly on any screen or printout.

How to use the Rotate PDF

  1. Upload your PDF: Choose the PDF file you want to rotate.
  2. Select rotation angle: Pick 90° clockwise, 180° or 270° clockwise.
  3. Download the rotated PDF: Click Rotate PDF and save the corrected file instantly.

Why use our Rotate PDF?
