Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Extract PDF Pages really free?

Yes. Extract PDF Pages on ToolsVera is 100% free to use with no signup, no hidden limits and no watermark added to your output.

How is this different from Split PDF?

Extract Pages builds one new PDF from the pages you pick. Split PDF can create multiple output files in one go.

Can I reorder pages while extracting?

Yes. Pages appear in the order you list them, so 5, 1, 3 outputs them in that sequence.

Are bookmarks and links preserved?

Links inside the kept pages are preserved. Global bookmarks are not carried over when only a subset is extracted.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Files are processed on the server and automatically deleted — we never store your content.
