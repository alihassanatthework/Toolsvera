Online PDF Editor — Edit PDF Like Word

Upload any text-based PDF and edit its content directly in a Word-style rich text editor. Change fonts, sizes, colors, headings, lists, and alignment. Insert images and links. Use Find & Replace to update text in bulk. Export your edited document back to PDF in one click.

How it works

  1. Upload your PDF — we extract the content into an editable view.
  2. Edit text, formatting, and layout with the Word-like toolbar.
  3. Use Find & Replace, Undo/Redo, and zoom as needed.
  4. Click Save & Export PDF to download.

FAQ

Will the exported PDF match the original layout exactly?

Content fidelity is high, layout fidelity is approximate. Complex multi-column or graphic-heavy PDFs simplify on export. For text-focused documents the result is very close.

Does it work on scanned PDFs?

Scanned PDFs contain images, not selectable text. OCR support is on the roadmap.

Is it free and private?

Yes. No signup, no watermark. Files are processed and deleted server-side.
