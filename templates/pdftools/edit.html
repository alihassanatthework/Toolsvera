Edit PDF Like a Word Document

Open any PDF and edit its text directly. ToolsVera reads each text fragment with its exact position, lets you click and rewrite it in place, then on Save it covers the original text and stamps your new text at the same coordinates. Use "Add text" to insert brand new content anywhere on a page.

How to edit a PDF

  1. Click Select PDF and upload your file.
  2. Click any word — type your replacement.
  3. Switch to + Add text to insert new content; click anywhere to drop a new text box.
  4. Click Save & Download.

FAQ

Will fonts match the original?

Edited text is rendered in Helvetica at the original size. For most text this looks very close to the original. Exotic fonts are not preserved.

Does it work for scanned PDFs?

No — scanned PDFs contain images, not real text. Use a PDF with selectable text.

Is it free?

Yes. Unlimited use, no signup, no watermark.
