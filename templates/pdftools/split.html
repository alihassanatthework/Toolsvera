Is this Split PDF really free?

Yes. Split PDF on ToolsVera is 100% free to use with no signup, no hidden limits and no watermark added to your output.

Can I split a large PDF into many small files?

Yes. Run the splitter once per range, or use a single range list to create multiple output PDFs.

No. Pages are copied directly from the source PDF without re-encoding, so quality is identical.
